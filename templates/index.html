FIELD GUIDE / DRAFT

Capture protocol.

A repeatable route for a non-engineer with an iPhone 15 or newer.

NOT YET FIELD-VALIDATED
!
Protocol status: draft

Photo and video paths use the built-in Camera app. Drive RGB-D archives are recognized, but the live LiDAR capture app, device-specific units, and field protocol still need verification; do not substitute a vendor floor plan for sensor data.

01 PHOTOS

Native Camera · stills

Per-room folders, no depth or pose data.

  1. Install nothing. Use the iPhone Camera app in Photo mode; turn flash off.
  2. Make one album folder per room. Take 4–6 stills, evenly spaced around the room.
  3. Keep the phone level at chest height. Overlap each view by about one third; include every corner and doorway.
  4. Hold still for a moment before each shot. Avoid people, mirrors, windows in direct glare, and ultra-wide zoom.
  5. Transfer originals without edits. Keep room folders intact, then select all folders in the intake form.
2–8 STILL IMAGES / ROOMANY iPHONE 15+
02 VIDEO

Native Camera · walkthrough

One continuous handheld clip per property.

  1. Use Video mode at 1×, 30 fps. Clean the lens and check free storage first.
  2. Start outside the first room. Walk slowly at a steady pace, pausing 2 seconds at each doorway.
  3. Pan each room once, keeping the horizon level; walk through connectors and return to the start if safe.
  4. Avoid fast turns, zooming, walking backwards, and abrupt exposure changes.
  5. Transfer the original .MOV or .MP4 file without compression and upload it as one capture.
ONE CONTINUOUS CLIPANY iPHONE 15+
03 LIDAR

Pro hardware · raw bundle recognized

The shared archives provide a usable sensor format; the live capture app and walk-in protocol remain unverified.

A valid bundle includes synchronized depth frames, odometry, and camera intrinsics. This app checks filenames only; it cannot verify units, calibration, timestamps, or sensor quality.
